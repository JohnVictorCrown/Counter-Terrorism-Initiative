Greetings,

I am writing on behalf of the Counter-Terrorism Initiative, an independent research project focused on human rights, accountability, and the rule of law in security operations across Brazil.

We are building a comprehensive, publicly accessible database that maps the landscape of human rights protections, oversight mechanisms, and civil-military relations in Brazil. Our goal is to support academic research, policy development, and civil society advocacy by making this information freely available.

For human rights organizations, we are seeking:
1. Collaboration on research and data sharing
2. Information about your monitoring, advocacy, and litigation programs
3. Partnership opportunities for joint projects and publications
4. Connections to your network of contacts in the human rights field
5. Any public reports, data, or resources your organization produces

For law enforcement and security agencies, we are interested in:
1. Public reports on community policing and human rights training
2. Transparency and accountability mechanisms in your institution
3. Civilian oversight programs and community engagement initiatives
4. Any publicly available data on use of force, complaints, and disciplinary actions
5. Collaboration opportunities for research on best practices in public security

We understand that some information may be operationally sensitive, and we are seeking only what is already publicly available or appropriate for release.

Could you please:
- Share any public reports, data sets, or resources your organization produces
- Let us know if you would be interested in research collaboration
- Direct us to the appropriate contact for partnership discussions

We believe that transparent, evidence-based research strengthens democratic institutions and human rights protections. We would welcome the opportunity to discuss our work with your team.

Thank you for your consideration.
